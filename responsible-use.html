Responsible use

You are more than a coin flip.

The answer on our homepage is completely random. It does not know you, your situation, your values, or what may be at stake.

What the answer can—and cannot—do

A random yes or no can be a moment of entertainment. It might even help you notice your own reaction: relief, disappointment, excitement, or hesitation. That reaction may be worth thinking about.

But the result cannot weigh evidence, understand consequences, predict an outcome, or decide what is right for you. It is not advice, and it should not be treated as a recommendation.

Important decisions

Do not leave high-stakes choices to randomness.

Never rely on this website for medical, legal, financial, safety, or emergency decisions. Pause and seek relevant facts and qualified help.

  • Consider your circumstances and the people affected.
  • Look for reliable, current information.
  • Talk with a qualified professional when appropriate.
  • In an emergency, contact local emergency services.

Your choice remains yours

You remain responsible for your choices and their consequences. That is not meant to add pressure; it is a reminder that your judgment, values, and capacity to ask for help matter more than an arbitrary result.

Sometimes the most responsible decision is to wait, gather more information, or ask someone with relevant expertise. Uncertainty is not failure. It is often part of choosing carefully.
